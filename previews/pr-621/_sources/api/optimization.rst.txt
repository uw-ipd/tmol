Optimization
============

The Cartesian and kinematic minimization entry points,
:func:`tmol.run_cart_min` and :func:`tmol.run_kin_min`, are imported from the
top-level package and documented in :doc:`top_level`. :mod:`tmol.optimization`
exports the score-function network used for kinematic minimization.
